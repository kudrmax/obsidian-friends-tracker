# People Tracker

Who you talked to and how, inside [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md). Every person is a note; every interaction is a line in its frontmatter.

- **The feed.** One list of what you logged, newest day first. Each row is a person and how you met: a text, a call or a meeting. Tap a row to change or delete it.
- **Two taps to log.** The round "+" opens a search over your people (the ones you logged last come first), then one row of buttons per choice: how, and for a meeting whether it was one on one or in company and whether it took the whole day; for a call whether it was a talk or a shared activity. A person you don't have yet is added right from the search.
- **In the note.** A person's note carries a panel above its text, drawn like Obsidian's Properties: days since you last met, the usual gap between your meetings, a year of months by format, the latest interactions.
- **Stats.** How much you talk to people this week, month or year and whether that grows; who you are falling behind with against your usual rhythm; who you see most.
- **Plain Markdown.** People are notes with a tag (or in a folder); interactions live in their frontmatter and sync with the vault.
